Working method

Clarity before commitments.

A limited scope and observable acceptance criteria are more useful than broad promises. This is the approach guiding our early service design.

Understand

Establish the actual problem, access requirements, expected outcome and risks.

Scope

Agree on a bounded deliverable, exclusions, acceptance conditions and price before starting.

Verify

Demonstrate what changed, which checks ran, and what remains outside the agreed scope.
